The room

Everything passes through the flame.

There is one heat source in this kitchen: a two-tonne oak-fired hearth we sweep and relight every afternoon. No induction, no gas, no sous-vide. Vegetables collapse in the embers, fish cures in the smoke path, bread proves on the mantel.

The menu is written at 3pm daily, after the market run, and printed once.
